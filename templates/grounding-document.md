# Grounding document

Your reusable summary. Paste it into every job-search chat so the AI knows who
it is talking to. Two layers: a labeled header a search agent can read the same
way every time, then the full version in your own voice.

Keep anything confidential or under NDA out of this file. Capture the shape of
the work, never the protected specifics.

---

## Quick-reference header (keep these exact labels)

- **Through-line:** [the one sentence that ties your work together]
- **Target titles:** [comma-separated; include the titles your role hides behind]
- **Must-haves:** [the things a role must have for you to say yes]
- **Dealbreakers:** [the things that make you pass, however good the rest is]
- **Comp floor:** [the base number below which it is a no]
- **Location + travel:** [base location, commute radius, travel you will accept]
- **Work authorization:** [status and any sponsorship needs]
- **Work arrangement:** [remote / hybrid / onsite, and what you actually want]
- **Level:** [seniority band you are targeting]

---

## The full version (in your voice)

**The work I most want to be doing.** [The shape of it, not just a title. What
energizes you, what you are genuinely great at, what you want more and less of.]

**My version of good.** [What makes a role a yes. What makes it a no. Be specific
enough that someone else could sort roles for you and mostly get it right.]

**My hard constraints.** [Comp floor, location and travel, work authorization,
and the things you will not do. Spell out the real limits.]

**What I bring that is hard to hire.** [The combination that is actually rare, in
plain terms, backed by something real.]

**Hidden range.** [Adjacent roles or titles you are probably qualified for but
would not think to search for.]

**How to use me.** [A line for future AI: read the header first, then this. Judge
fit honestly against the must-haves and dealbreakers. Write in my voice.]
