# Career blueprint

Your private source of truth, not a resume. Length does not matter, completeness
does. You never send this. You mine it to write tailored resumes and to feed your
search. Build it by having the AI interview you, one question at a time.

Keep anything confidential or under NDA out of this file. Record the shape of the
work, never the client name or the private numbers.
