# Search spec

The instructions a daily search agent uses to find roles that fit you. Write it
from your grounding document. Keep the field labels and the rubric intact so any
AI applies it the same way every day.

---

- **Shape of role:** [what the work actually is, in one or two lines]
- **Titles it hides behind:** [comma-separated real-world titles to search]
- **Signals of a real match:** [responsibilities or phrases in a posting that
  mean it is genuinely your kind of role, not just a title match]
- **Must-haves:** [every condition a role must meet to survive the filter]
- **Hard dealbreakers:** [comp floor, location and travel, work authorization,
  and anything else that is an automatic no]
- **Sources to rotate:** [job boards, company pages, networks worth checking;
  rotate so the same ones are not hit every day]

---

## Fit rubric (score each role 1 to 5)

Adjust the wording to you, but keep every level spelled out so the score means
the same thing every run.

- **5:** clears every must-have and shows at least two signals of strong fit.
- **4:** clears every must-have, no dealbreakers, a solid fit but not exceptional.
- **3:** clears the must-haves but has one real gap.
- **2:** borderline; a must-have is only partly met.
- **1:** trips a dealbreaker or misses a must-have.

For each surfaced role, record the score, one honest line on why it fits, and one
line on the biggest gap or risk.
